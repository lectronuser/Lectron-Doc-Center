    Lectron M9N GPS

    GNSS Module with Compass

    In design

    This product is currently in the design phase. Specifications may change before release.

    u-blox M9N GNSS module with compass and UART & I2C interface.

    Specification

    Feature Lectron M9N GPS
    Main Processor u-blox M9N
    Protocol BeiDou, Galileo, GLONASS, GNSS, GPS
    Frequency 1.575 GHz, 1.602 GHz
    Data Rate 921.6 kbps
    Sensitivity -167 dBm
    Compass IST8310
    Connector 4 Pin JST GH
    Interface UART & I2C
    Power Input 5V
    Button Function Safety Switch In
    LED Functions Safety, GPS Fix, RGB(Status)
    RGB Led Driver IS31FL3195
    Buzzer Yes
    Operating Temperature -40°C ~ 85°C
    Weight ~ 20 g
    Dimensions 35 × 35 × 14 mm

    Dimensions
    Size (W × L × H) 35 × 35 × 14 mm
    Weight ~ 20 g

    All dimensions in mm. Outline drawing of the overall envelope; a detailed mechanical drawing is not yet available.
