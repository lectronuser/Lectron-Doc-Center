# Lectron H7 FPV Pro

## **High-Performance FPV Flight Controller**

The Lectron H7 FPV Pro is a compact flight controller built around the STM32H743 processor, designed for FPV and racing platforms that need a small footprint without sacrificing sensor quality or software flexibility.

It supports a wide flight battery voltage range and CAN connectivity, making it a strong choice for performance-oriented FPV builds that go beyond entry-level flight controllers.

## **Product Description**

Lectron H7 FPV Pro combines a high-performance FMU with a broad software ecosystem, supporting PX4, ArduPilot, iNav, and Betaflight. This flexibility allows pilots and integrators to choose the firmware that best fits their platform and flying style.

Its 36 × 36 mm mounting footprint and lightweight aluminum case make it easy to integrate into FPV racing and freestyle frames, while CAN support enables integration with peripheral devices.
